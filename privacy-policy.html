Wellington — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

Overview

Wellington is a health data export tool for iOS. Your privacy is fundamental to how this app is built — your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share or save it.

Health Data

Wellington requests read-only access to Apple Health data categories you select (such as steps, heart rate, sleep, workouts, and others). This data is used solely to generate export files in the format you choose (CSV, JSON, or Markdown).

  • Health data is processed entirely on your device.
  • No health data is sent to any server, analytics service, or third party.
  • Exported files are stored locally or in your personal iCloud Drive — only you have access.

Data Collection

Wellington does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.
  • No user accounts or registration required.
  • No network requests are made (except for optional In-App Purchases processed by Apple).

In-App Purchases

Wellington offers optional tip jar purchases. These are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The developer does not receive any personal or payment information from these transactions.

iCloud

If you enable automatic saving to iCloud Drive, exported files are stored in your personal iCloud container. This data is protected by your Apple ID and iCloud encryption. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

Background Export

If you enable scheduled exports, the app processes health data in the background and saves files to iCloud Drive. No data is sent externally during this process.

Children's Privacy

Wellington does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the App Store listing.
